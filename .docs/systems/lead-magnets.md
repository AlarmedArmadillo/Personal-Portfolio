# Lead Magnets & Money Models — Playbook

Source: Alex Hormozi's *$100M Leads* / *$100M Money Models* ("Engage Your Leads" module), walked through slide by slide and adapted to this brand.

**Keep these principles intact when making content and business decisions.** They govern how we build offers, lead magnets, funnels, and the content that feeds them.

---

## TL;DR
A lead magnet is a **complete solution to one narrow problem, given free/cheap to attract ideal customers** — designed backward from the core offer so that solving the narrow problem *reveals the next problem, solved only by the core offer.* Great leads want great lead magnets: quality in, quality out.

---

## The build sequence (in order)

1. **Start with the Problem–Solution Cycle.** Design backward from the core offer. Every solution reveals a new problem — an endless cycle. The giveaway must reveal a next problem that *only the core offer solves.*
2. **Who + the narrow problem.** One ideal customer, one specific, meaningful problem. Narrow, not broad.
3. **How to solve it — pick one of 3 types:**
   - **Reveal the problem** (a diagnosis) — best for problems that *get worse while you wait*. e.g. an audit, a calculator, a scorecard.
   - **Free trial / sample** — a taste. Limit uses, time, or both.
   - **Free step 1 of X** — give the first step of a multi-step process free; sell the rest.
4. **How to deliver it — pick a mechanism that fits the cycle:**
   - **Software** — give a tool (spreadsheet, calculator, dashboard).
   - **Information** — teach something (guide, mini-course, swipe file, video).
   - **Services** — do work for free (audit, first step done for them).
   - **Physical** — something they can hold. (Rarely worth the friction for a digital business.)
5. **Name it.** Framing matters **most for lead generation** (how many opt in). Use the **MAGIC** shape — Magnetic reason · Avatar · Goal · Interval (time) · Container word (email/checklist/calculator). **A/B test the name** before committing (poll it). "After you've written your headline you've spent 80 cents of your dollar." — Ogilvy.
6. **Make it easy to consume.** Frictionless. Every format the avatar wants, phone-first, instant delivery, no form maze. A lead magnet nobody consumes is worth zero.
7. **Make it good — zero fluff.** Give away the *secrets*; sell the *implementation*. Free and paid must both be exceptional.
8. **CTA — make it easy to ask for more.** Clear, simple, direct language + a real reason to act now (urgency / scarcity / cost-of-delay). Any reason beats no reason — but ours must be true.

### The 4 marks of a great lead magnet
1. Engages ideal customers when they see it.
2. Gets more people to engage than the core offer alone.
3. Is valuable enough that people actually consume it.
4. Makes the right people more likely to buy.

**End result:** more interest, more revenue, stronger brand — at the same time.

## The Money Model

The order and structure in which you get paid, designed so **winning one customer funds finding the next** (client-financed acquisition).

- **Attraction offer** — win the customer (free/cheap; the lead magnet lives here).
- **Upsell** — raise 30-day revenue; this is where the margin is made.
- **Downsell** — convert the people who said no with a cheaper/different version.
- **Continuity** — recurring revenue; the long-term profit.

**The test of a good money model:** a new customer pays for themselves within ~30 days (ideally 2×). If yes, acquisition is self-funding and you can scale without limit. If no, you're capped.

## Alex Hormozi — Teachings, Methodologies & Philosophies

The quotable principles behind the frameworks above:

- **Give away the secrets, sell the implementation.** The knowledge is free; the *doing it for them* (or the speed) is the paid thing. Never fear "giving too much away."
- **The market judges you on your FREE stuff, not your paid.** 99% who see you will never buy — but the free value builds the reputation that converts the rest and earns referrals.
- **People buy on the expectation of future value.** The easiest way to make someone predict they'll get value *after* they buy is to give them value *before*. Provide value before they pay.
- **A person who pays with their time now is more likely to pay with their money later.** Attention is the first payment. (Why comment-to-DM / opt-in mechanics work.)
- **Every problem solved reveals a new problem** — a never-ending Problem–Solution Cycle. This is both the funnel logic *and* the content engine (a post solves one problem and teases the next).
- **A lead magnet + core offer beats the core offer alone.** Same traffic, more trust, more sales (his example: 1 sale vs 3 sales out of 25). "Be a legal drug dealer" — give a real taste that leads to the full thing.
- **Naming/framing drives lead generation; quality drives conversion.** Both matter, different jobs. Test names like you'd test anything.
- **Any reason to act beats no reason** (the "because" effect) — but keep it honest.
- **Win a customer so cheaply that winning them funds finding the next one** (client-financed acquisition).
- **Great leads want great lead magnets.** Quality of the magnet sets the quality of the leads. The right people over the most people.

## How we apply it (this brand)

Our adaptations — where Hormozi's framework meets our proof-first, principles-first positioning:

- **Universal lesson, specific example.** We are a growth partner teaching principles that apply to *any* business — but every piece of content is anchored in one concrete case (ecom cart email today, a coach's no-show follow-up next week). The specific is the hook; the principle is the payoff. **Niche is the door; principles are the house.** We earn the right to be broad by being specific first.
- **Honest scarcity only.** Our real constraint — it's the founder plus one assistant — is our most credible reason to act now. We cap our done-for-you work to protect quality and say so plainly. **Never fake urgency; fake scarcity is poison to a proof-first brand.**
- **Give the secrets freely.** This *is* our no-fluff voice. The core offer is **implementation** (done-for-you / the systems built), never the withheld information.
- **The quality filter starts at the content, not the magnet.** Great content → great leads → great lead magnet → great customers. Generic content pulls freebie-seekers, so every post must pass: *"what specific thing does this help a specific person do?"*
- **Guardrail for principles-first content:** the *lesson* can be universal, but the *example* never can be.

### Our funnel in Hormozi's language
```
Content (great, specific)  →  attracts great leads
Lead magnet (free: 1 template + the secrets)   →  Attraction
£27 pack (all templates, productized)           →  Entry / upsell
Done-for-you service (implementation)           →  Core offer (most money)
Retainer / ongoing                              →  Continuity
```

## Worked example — our first lead magnet

**"The 10-Minute Cart Email That Recovers Lost Sales"** (Info delivery · Free step 1 of X · ecom beachhead)

- **Free:** one copy-paste cart-recovery email + a 60-sec Loom giving the real secrets (send at 1h/24h/48h; put the discount in email 2–3, never email 1; keep a "reply to me" line).
- **Bridge (reveals next problem):** "This is 1 of the 5 emails every store needs. Want the other four?"
- **Handoff:** → £27 full-flow pack → done-for-you service.
- **CTA:** "Comment CART and I'll DM you the email. Every night without it is carts walking out the door."

Delivered as the specific example; taught as the universal principle (*your marketing should sell for you while you sleep — here's the smallest version of that, and it works for any business with a checkout or a booking page*).
