# Carousel — Free vs Paid Lead Magnets

First flagship lead-magnet carousel. Shares the unified CTA: **comment FUNNEL** → the Blueprint.

## Delivery (Hormozi money model)
- **This carousel = traffic / top-of-funnel content.** Post as **native swipeable images** (PNG/JPG exported from Canva) — IG carousel / Threads multi-image. NOT a public PDF (native posts get the reach; the Canva PDF is just the source file).
- **The lead magnet (attraction offer) = The Lead Magnet Blueprint** (`lead-magnet-blueprint.md`), delivered as **Information** — a free **Notion doc** (instant, updatable). This is what people get when they comment FUNNEL. Notion > PDF (link-based, updatable, holds templates + a tool).
- **The ladder:** carousel (traffic) → Blueprint (free attraction) → £27 template pack (upsell) → done-for-you service (core) → retainer (continuity).
- **Give the secret / sell the implementation:** the carousel gives the *principles* (the what); the Blueprint gives the *toolkit* (templates, AI prompt, 3-email sequence, worked examples — the how/do). No overlap — different jobs.

## Format
- **Full teaching version:** ~15 cards (below).
- **Tight version (reel or short carousel):** cut to ~8 — slides 1, 6, 10, 11, 12, 13, 14, 15 (hook, the rule, the 3 types, the table, the trap, the hinge, the wink, the CTA).
