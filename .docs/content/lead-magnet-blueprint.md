## The Lead Magnet Blueprint
*The exact system I use to turn attention into leads — and leads into sales. Free. No fluff.*

**Read your lane:** 🎥 creator · 💻 digital product seller · 🛒 ecom store owner. Principles are the same for everyone — only the specifics change. That's the whole point.

### The big picture (same for every business)
**TRAFFIC → LEAD MAGNET → AUTOMATION → SALES**
- **Traffic** = attention (your content).
- **Lead magnet** = turns attention into an email you *own*.
- **Automation** = emails that build trust while you sleep.
- **Sales** = the offer, to people who already trust you.

The lead magnet is the **hinge**. Skip it and you sell to strangers. Nail it and every other stage works better.

### Part 1 — What a lead magnet actually is
> A **complete solution to one narrow problem**, given free.

- ❌ **The mistake (everyone):** a 47-slide "Ultimate Guide." That's homework, not a gift.
- ✅ **The fix:** give a *shortcut* they can use in 5 minutes.

**What "good" looks like for you:**
- 🎥 "Steal my 5 hooks that got 2M views" (swipe file)
- 💻 "The 1-page checklist from inside my course" (a real slice of it)
- 🛒 "The email that wins back abandoned carts"

**The golden rule (universal):** *give away the secrets, sell the implementation.* Knowledge is free; doing it for them (or fast) is paid. People judge your paid stuff by how good your free stuff is. *(Bustamante gives away his whole 6-figure launch playbook + every email — and sells the done-for-you agency.)*

### Part 2 — The 3 types (pick one)
1. **Reveal the problem** (a diagnosis) — for problems that get worse while you wait.
   - 🎥 "Score your hook in 10 seconds" · 💻 "Is your offer actually clear? 5-question test" · 🛒 "How much revenue your emails are leaving on the table"
2. **Free sample** — a taste of the paid thing.
   - 🎥 one preset/template · 💻 one free lesson · 🛒 a sample or first-order perk
3. **Free step 1 of X** — give the first step; sell the rest.
   - 🎥 step 1 of your content system · 💻 module 1 free · 🛒 the first email of the flow

### Part 3 — Build it (the 7 steps, universal)
1. **Narrow problem** — one specific, painful thing.
2. **Solve it** — pick a type above.
3. **Deliver it** — doc, template, calculator, or short video.
4. **Name it** — drives sign-ups more than anything. Test 2 names.
5. **Make it easy** — usable on a phone, in minutes, no maze.
6. **Make it good** — real value, zero fluff.
7. **Point to the next step** — reveal the problem only your paid offer solves.

**Sharpen it → micro-magnets:** smaller wins beat fat ebooks. One tiny problem, solved instantly.

### Part 4 — Plug it into the funnel
- **Traffic (universal):** content's job is to *drive people to the magnet.* Great content → great leads; hypey content → freebie-seekers who never buy.
- **Automation (same shape, different trigger):** a simple 3-email sequence — **1:** deliver + welcome · **2:** a quick win + your story · **3:** the offer. Set once, runs forever.
  - Trigger differs: 🎥 opt-in from a post · 💻 opt-in or after a free lesson · 🛒 opt-in, browse, or cart.
- **Sales (universal):** the magnet solved one problem and revealed the next — your paid offer solves *that*. The sequence sells; you don't hard-pitch.

### Part 5 — Steal these (adapt the specifics)
**Comment-to-DM script (works for everyone):**
*"Comment [WORD] and I'll send you [the thing]. Every day without it is [cost of waiting]."*

**The bridge line (universal):** *"This is 1 of [X]. Want the rest done? → [offer]."*

**AI prompt (fills itself in for any business):**
*"I run a [type of business]. My customers struggle with [problem]. Give me 3 micro-lead-magnet ideas that solve one narrow piece of that in under 5 minutes, each pointing toward [my paid offer]."*
