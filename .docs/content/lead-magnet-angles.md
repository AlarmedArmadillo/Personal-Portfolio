# Lead-Magnet Content Angles — Engine

Content built on the Hormozi lead-magnet principles (see `.docs/systems/lead-magnets.md`).
One framework → ~12 principle clusters → each spins 3–5 videos at different specificity →
each can drop its own **micro-magnet** (comment a keyword → a 5-min written breakdown of that one principle).

**This is a living doc.** As we learn more principles, add clusters. As angles get approved, star them.

## Rules
- **Universal lesson, specific example.** Open on a concrete case (an ecom store, a coach, an agency); land on the universal principle. Broad positioning, specific content.
- **Honest voice.** No hype, no fake scarcity. Give the secrets away.
- **The recursion is the flex.** These videos teach the method *and are* the method (comment → get the magnet).
- Hook tags: 🎓 broad/educational · 🔥 contrarian · 📊 specific/authority · 📖 story.

## The clusters

### 1. What a lead magnet actually is (complete solution to a narrow problem)
*Teaches:* the free thing must fully solve ONE small problem, not tease.
- 🎓 "This is some digital-marketing advice universities don't teach you."
- 🔥 "Your free thing isn't working because it's a brochure, not a gift."
- 📊 "The 1 rule that makes a freebie actually get you customers."
*Micro-magnet:* Comment **MAGNET** → the 5-min written breakdown of the whole framework.

### 2. Give away the secrets, sell the implementation
*Teaches:* the knowledge is free; the doing is paid.
- 🔥 "Stop hiding your best stuff. Give it ALL away — here's why you'll make more."
- 🎓 "Why the smartest businesses tell you exactly how they do it."
- 📖 "I gave away my whole process for free. It made me more money, not less."
*Micro-magnet:* Comment **SECRETS** → "what to give free vs what to charge for" cheat sheet.

### 3. The market judges you on your FREE stuff
*Teaches:* people predict your paid quality from your free quality.
- 🔥 "People decide if they'll buy from you based on the stuff you give away for free."
- 📊 "Why your free content is the most important sales tool you have."
*Micro-magnet:* Comment **FREE** → the "make your freebie exceptional" checklist.

### 4. Value before they buy
*Teaches:* give value first so they predict future value.
- 🎓 "The easiest way to get someone to buy: help them before they pay you a cent."
- 📖 "I stopped pitching and started helping first. Sales went up."
*Micro-magnet:* Comment **VALUE** → 3 ways to deliver value before the sale.

### 5. The problem–solution cycle
*Teaches:* every solution reveals a new problem — endless content + endless offers.
- 🎓 "Why you'll never run out of things to sell (or post about)."
- 🔥 "You don't have a content problem. You have a 'you don't understand the cycle' problem."
*Micro-magnet:* Comment **CYCLE** → the problem→solution map for your niche.

### 6. Lead magnet + core offer beats pitching alone
*Teaches:* same traffic, more trust, more sales (1 vs 3 of 25).
- 📊 "Same 25 people. One got 1 sale, the other got 3. Here's the difference."
- 🔥 "Pitching your main offer cold is why nobody's buying."
*Micro-magnet:* Comment **BRIDGE** → the "freebie → offer" bridge template.

### 7. Naming matters most (80 cents on the headline)
*Teaches:* the name drives opt-ins; quality drives conversion.
- 🎓 "You spend 80% of your effort the moment you write the title. Most people wing it."
- 📊 "I A/B tested 3 names for the same freebie. The winner got 2× the sign-ups."
*Micro-magnet:* Comment **NAME** → the MAGIC naming formula + 10 examples.

### 8. Lead magnets are dead → micro-magnets
*Teaches:* same principle, sharper — one tiny problem, instantly consumed.
- 🔥 "Lead magnets are dead." *(bait, then teach micro-magnets)*
- 🎓 "Why a 1-page freebie beats your 40-slide PDF every time."
*Micro-magnet:* Comment **MICRO** → 5 micro-magnet ideas you can make today.

### 9. The 3 types (reveal / sample / step 1)
*Teaches:* pick how you solve the narrow problem.
- 🎓 "There are only 3 kinds of freebie that actually work. Here they are."
- 📊 "Here's exactly which freebie to make for your type of business."
*Micro-magnet:* Comment **TYPES** → the 3-types picker (which one fits you).

### 10. Make it good — no fluff
*Teaches:* fluff repels the people who would've bought.
- 🔥 "Your fluff is quietly costing you customers."
- 🎓 "Here are 8 marketing lessons that took me 8 years to learn."
*Micro-magnet:* Comment **FLUFF** → the "cut the fluff" content checklist.

### 11. Honest scarcity / a real reason to act now
*Teaches:* any reason beats none — but fake urgency kills trust.
- 🔥 "Fake urgency is why people don't trust marketers. Do this instead."
- 📖 "I only take a few clients a month — and I tell people exactly why."
*Micro-magnet:* Comment **NOW** → 5 honest reasons-to-act-now (no fake timers).

### 12. Client-financed acquisition (the money model)
*Teaches:* win a customer so cheaply they fund the next.
- 🎓 "The math that lets some businesses outspend everyone: they get paid to get customers."
- 📊 "If a customer pays for themselves in 30 days, you can grow forever. Here's how."
*Micro-magnet:* Comment **MODEL** → the 30-day payback calculator/worksheet.
