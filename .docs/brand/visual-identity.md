# Visual Identity

The feel: **witty, friendly, business-savvy.** Wit lives in the *copy*; the design stays clean and confident. Orange on warm off-white — high contrast, no clutter.

## Colours
| Name | Hex | Role |
|------|-----|------|
| **Carbon Black** | `#1A1A1A` | All body / reading text; strong statements. Never pure `#000`. |
| **Chalk** | `#F4F9E9` | Primary background — soft off-white (never pure `#FFF`; softer, warmer, less glare). |
| **Crimson Carrot** | `#F5540A` | Primary accent — CTAs, buttons, the highlighted caption word, key emphasis. |
| **Tiger Orange** | `#F18701` | Secondary / warm accent — supporting highlights, panel tints, friendly warmth. |

### Colour rules
- **Orange is an accent, never small body text** — it fails contrast on a light background. Body/reading text is always **Carbon Black on Chalk** (excellent contrast).
- When orange *must* be display text, use **Crimson Carrot** at large, bold sizes only. Keep **Tiger Orange** for fills and warmth, not text.
- **Never** pure white (`#FFFFFF`) or pure black (`#000000`).
- **One accent per view.** Don't let both oranges fight in the same block — pick one to lead.

## Fonts (max 2)
- **Headlines / captions:** **Poppins** (Bold / ExtraBold) — friendly, geometric, reads great in caps.
- **Body:** **Inter** (Regular / Medium) — clean, credible, readable.
- Both are free (Google Fonts + Canva), so they're brand-kit ready.
- *(Working default. If we want more wit in headlines later, a soft display like Fraunces is the swap — one-line change.)*

## The look
- Warm off-white (Chalk) backgrounds, Carbon text, orange to point the eye.
- Big confident type, generous whitespace, one accent.
- **No** gradients, clipart, stock-corporate imagery, or decorative drop shadows.
- **Wit comes from the words, not the decoration.** The design stays clean and savvy; the personality is in the copy.

## Content captions (short-form video)
- Bold uppercase; the key word highlighted in **Crimson Carrot** (this replaces the earlier yellow — orange makes it ours).
- Carbon text on Chalk, or inverted (Chalk text on Carbon) for pattern-interrupt slides.

## Do / Don't
- ✅ Orange CTA button · Carbon text · Chalk background.
- ✅ One Crimson-Carrot highlight word per caption.
- ❌ Orange body paragraphs · ❌ pure white / pure black · ❌ two oranges competing · ❌ gradients & clipart.
