## Quick Context

Simon owns a 20+ year roofing business. Traffic has declined because his lead source (word-of-mouth + PPC) is shrinking. He lacks an organic acquisition engine entirely.

**The Plan:** Build a predictable lead-generation system using proof-first positioning, local SEO, paid funnels, and email nurture—applying Mango Media's systems thinking to local tradesman marketing.

**Critical Blocker:** IONOS hosting does not allow direct edits to titles, meta tags, or schema markup without a full site migration. This blocks Phase 1's on-page SEO work (steps 8, 10, 12).

**Solution:** Reorder Phase 1 to maximize off-site SEO, paid funnel infrastructure, and content authority **while avoiding IONOS constraints**. On-page optimization deferred to Phase 2 (if migration is justified by Phase 1 results).

## Brand & Systems Framework

See `brand-systems-synthesis.md` for full details. Key points:

### Positioning
**From:** "We fix roofs."  
**To:** "The roofing expert who shows his work."

- Proof-first: case studies, before/afters, process videos
- Direct tone: straightforward answers, no fluff
- Accessible language: explain roofing without jargon overload
- Authority content: Threads, GBP posts, local backlinks

### The Funnel
```
GBP + Google visibility + Threads content
        ↓
Lead capture (60-second quote quiz, contact form)
        ↓
Email/SMS nurture (inspection findings, material options, financing)
        ↓
High-ticket roofing sale (£3k–£20k average)
        ↓
Repeat/referral (maintenance reminders, seasonal upsells)
```

### Voice & Tone
- **Direct, grounded, honest** — no corporate BS
- **5th grade reading level** — homeowners are intelligent but busy
- **Show, don't tell** — before/afters, real projects, actual costs
- **Lead with clarity** — customer problems, solution options, transparent pricing

## Migration Decision Framework

**Migrate to new host NOW if:**
- Willing to invest 2–4 weeks in Webflow/WordPress setup
- Want full control of titles, schema, site structure immediately
- Budget exists (~£50–150/month recurring)
- Sees quick wins from Phase 1 (justifies the effort)

**Migrate LATER (Phase 2) if:**
- Phase 1 paid funnel is working (proof of demand)
- Off-site SEO + GBP is carrying the load (often true for local)
- Prefers to prove ROI before investing in site rebuild

**Recommendation:** **Proceed with Phase 1 as described.** IONOS constraints don't block the highest-ROI work. If LSA converts well and GBP is ranking, migration becomes optional—off-page can sustain growth. If Phase 1 flatlines, migration becomes urgent (on-page SEO needed to unlock organic).
